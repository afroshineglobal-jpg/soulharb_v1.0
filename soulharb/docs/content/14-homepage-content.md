# 14. Improved homepage content

The draft says "No AI" six times and "earn income" three times, but never says how long the process takes, what it costs, what listeners do and do not do, or how data is handled. The rewrite keeps the human-first voice, says each thing once, replaces outcome claims with mission statements, and answers the questions an applicant has before they commit. Text in square brackets is for you to fill in.

### 14.1 Structure

1. Hero with one primary action
2. Why human listening
3. How it works, with time per step
4. Who we are looking for
5. What listeners do, and what they do not
6. Earning, stated honestly
7. How we keep everyone safe
8. Your ID and your data
9. Questions
10. Final call to action

### 14.2 Copy

**Page title:** Become a SoulHarb Listener | Verified human listeners

**Description tag:** Apply to become a verified, trained SoulHarb listener. Real people, real conversations, no bots.

**Hero**

# Be the person who actually listens.

SoulHarb connects people who need to be heard with verified, trained human listeners. Every conversation is between two real people, with no bots and no scripts.

Primary button: **Start your application**. Secondary link: Log in.

Beneath the buttons: Free to apply. About 20 minutes to begin. \[Training fee statement, for example: Training is free.]

**Why human listening**

AI can answer questions. It cannot sit with you while you work out what you feel. Many people carry a worry, a loss or a long day with nobody to tell. SoulHarb exists so that someone is there, and that someone is a person.

**How it works**

| Step | What happens | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Watch and apply | A short film, then your account and profile | 20 minutes |
| 2. Questionnaire | Scenarios that show how you listen | 20 minutes |
| 3. Verify your identity | A government ID and a selfie, reviewed by our team and deleted after review | 10 minutes, review in \[X] days |
| 4. Interview | A 30-minute video conversation with one of our team | 30 minutes |
| 5. Training | \[Format and length] on listening, boundaries and safety | \[1 hour] |
| 6. Welcome | You become a verified SoulHarb listener |  |

**Who we are looking for**

You do not need a psychology degree. You do need to be 18 or over, patient, honest, and able to listen without judging or fixing. You keep what you hear confidential, and you can follow clear boundaries. Experience in caring roles helps, and life experience counts.

**What listeners do, and what they do not**

Listeners give a person their full attention, help them feel heard, and know when to point toward other help. Listeners are not therapists, doctors or advisers. They do not diagnose, treat, give medical, legal or financial advice, or take conversations off the platform. SoulHarb is not a crisis service; if someone is in danger, listeners follow the safety steps we teach.

**Earning, stated honestly**

Listeners will be paid for their time once conversations open to the public. \[Rate or range and how payment works.] We cannot promise a fixed income or a number of hours, and we will publish rates before you begin training. \[Fee statement.]

**How we keep everyone safe**

Every listener is identity-checked, interviewed by a person, trained, and approved by our team before speaking to anyone. Listeners agree to a code of conduct, and anyone can report a concern at any time through the app.

**Your ID and your data**

We use your ID only to confirm who you are. Only trained reviewers can see it, every view is recorded, and the images are deleted 30 days after we decide. Read the privacy notice for the full detail.

Apply to become a SoulHarb listener. It is free to start.

### 14.3 Notes on the changes

- Removed claims that listening reduces loneliness or builds a calmer society; these read as outcome promises. The mission statement carries the same feeling without the claim.
- Added fee, timing and ID handling up front, because hiding them damages trust and raises drop-off later.
- Added the boundaries line so the page does not attract people looking for counselling work.
- Every bracketed item must be filled in and approved before launch; do not publish earnings language without legal review.
