# ADR 0007: Launch markets and languages

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20

## Context

Source documents were unclear on markets and languages.

## Decision

Seeker launch markets are the US, India and Nigeria; listener recruitment is global. Languages are English and Brazilian Portuguese. Brazil is not a seeker market, so confirm that Brazilian Portuguese is a listener recruitment language.

## Consequences

Drives accepted ID types per country, legal review, i18n setup in packages/i18n and reviewer coverage for both languages.
