# ADR 0008: Free training during MVP and Beta

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20

## Context

A paid training fee for a job that promises income looks like an advance-fee scam.

## Decision

Training is free during MVP and Beta. Self-paced modules are mandatory; a weekly live Q&A is optional.

## Consequences

No training payments to build in Phase 1. Revisit pricing, or refunds as payout credit, with the payouts phase.
